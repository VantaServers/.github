## What we build

Nearly everything you touch in Vanta RP was made by us. The server, the website and the Discord bot are all built
in-house, and they talk to each other: buy something on the website and it lands in your inventory, even if
you're offline.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🏙️ The city</h3>
      More than <b>30 custom addons</b> on one shared library, so the whole server feels like one game: mining,
      fishing, factions, a bank with real ATMs, a marketplace, quests and skills, crates, custom jobs, and a day and
      night cycle with its own weather.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🌐 The website</h3>
      A <b>live 3D city map</b> rendered from the server's own map file, the in-game marketplace on the web, city mail,
      leaderboards and player profiles, forums, an events calendar, and <b>My Vanta</b>, every player's own
      dashboard.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🤖 VantaBot</h3>
      Our Discord bot: tickets, staff applications, suggestions and moderation shields. It's run from the website's
      panel and kept in sync with the game, so a warning given in Discord counts in the city too.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Inside the city

| | |
|---|---|
| 💰 **Make your fortune** | Mine ore, land a record catch, crack the bank vault or trade on the marketplace |
| 🚩 **Run a faction** | Your own name, colour and emblem, with ranks, a shared bank and vault, and wars for a stake |
| 🛠️ **Create your job** | Design a custom job with its own models, loadout and friends list |
| 📈 **Level up** | Quests, skill trees, daily spins and a Player of the Week, chosen every Monday |
| 🌦️ **Live in it** | A 48-minute day, rain and clear skies, snow every December, and payday at 22:00 |
| 🤝 **Play together** | Community goals the whole city works towards, with everyone who helped getting paid |
